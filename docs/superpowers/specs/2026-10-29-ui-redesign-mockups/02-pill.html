STEPS + SUMMARY

One question at a time, then a clean summary

  • Adding asks five short questions, one per screen: name and photo → type and amount → times → how long → check and save.
  • Opening a pill shows a summary with her photo. Tap any line to change just that line.
  • Harder to get wrong. Easier for a first-timer, or for her.

Either way: times show as morning, afternoon and night with the sun and moon. A sound belongs to a time, so changing the 9:00 PM sound changes it for every pill at 9:00 PM, because they ring together. The same page opens on a relative's phone as "Amma's pill".
